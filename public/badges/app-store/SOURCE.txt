Official Apple App Store badge artwork
======================================

Source: Apple App Store Marketing Tools
  https://toolbox.marketingtools.apple.com/
Guidelines:
  https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/

Files are unmodified SVGs returned by:
  /api/v2/badges/download-on-the-app-store/{black|white}/{lang}?size=250x83

Locale map used by this site:
  en → en-us
  tr → tr-tr
  de → de-de
  fr → fr-fr
  it → it-it
  zh → zh-cn (Simplified Chinese)
  ja → ja-jp

Do not edit paths/text in these SVGs. Re-download from Apple if assets change.
